# LAST RESORT: engine test story
*Developed live on Nov 25, 2026 from a one-line logline. Spine and movements locked; beat sheet v1.*

## Locked
- **Tone:** Coen-style crime comedy (ordinary people, real danger, comedy from character). About 100 min.
- **Crime mechanism:** the timeshare paperwork Gary signs for prizes makes the Pruitts the legal owners and directors of a crime ring's shell companies.
- **Couple:** Gary lives for freebies ("Sign everything, buy nothing"). Donna has had enough after 30 years of a deferred life, and she reads the fine print.
- **Pressure:** both the ring and the feds.
- **The TV** is the hub of a secret mesh network the ring is deploying across the States.
- **The ring** is a global "mob" of tech nerds; the shell companies are the laundering layer.
- **Nodes:** only some circuit members are nodes. Finding out which ones is part of the investigation.
- **Kyle,** the pitch rep at the Canadian resort, is a fellow victim trapped working for the ring.
- **Agent Halvorsen** (placeholder name) is a loon posing as a straight arrow (past lives, simulation theory, strange fixations). He stays a pure comic foil, and his straight-arrow act cracks at the right moment.
- **Polly Vance** (placeholder name) is a beloved middle-school teacher on the circuit and the ring's recruiter. The nerds are her former coding-club students, and she's still protecting "her kids." Her arc is choosing a side, and she delivers the sting pitch.
- **Climax** runs through the circuit, Gary's network of prize hunters.

## Spine
- **Gary** wants something for nothing. He needs to learn that his system isn't thrift, it's a way of never committing to anything.
- **Donna** wants one real vacation they actually pay for.
- **Theme:** nothing is free; the fine print always collects.
- **Central question:** can they get out of a deal they signed without paying the price?
- **Ending:** they pay full price for something that matters, together.
- **Proposed (not locked):** the ring targets professional freeloaders on purpose (prolific signers, long paper trails, no assets).

## Beat sheet v1

### 1. The Circuit (0–12 min)
1. **Cold open (0–4):** Gary and Donna take apart a timeshare closer in Branson with practiced moves and walk out with two cruise vouchers that are blacked out every day of the year.
2. **The circuit (4–8):** the circuit's monthly "birthday" meal at a buffet (someone's birthday every month means a free entrée). We meet the rivalries, the scams, and Polly, the warmest person in the room. Three members brag about the huge free TVs they recently got *(plants the nodes)*.
3. **Home (8–10):** Gary's garage full of unopened prizes. Donna's Amalfi Coast brochure, hidden in a cookbook.
4. **The tip (10–12):** Polly tips Gary off to the best prize ever, an 85-inch TV that has to be picked up in person at a resort in Ontario. Gary sells it to Donna as "a vacation." She goes because it's the closest she'll get.

### 2. North (12–30 min)
5. **The road (12–17):** a road trip run on cheapness: a 3-hour detour to avoid a $6 toll, hoarding hotel breakfasts. Donna's small, quiet moments of wanting more.
6. **The pitch (17–24):** an empty off-season resort. Kyle sweats through his pitch, glancing at a camera in the corner; Donna notices. Gary signs an absurdly tall stack of forms. Kyle almost warns them, then doesn't.
7. **The prize (24–27):** the TV is enormous. They make it fit.
8. **The border (27–30):** border agents scan the TV and wave it through. Triumph.

### 3. The Fine Print (30–50 min)
9. **It's alive (30–33):** the TV turns itself on at 3 a.m. and the router lights go wild. Gary decides it's a bonus feature.
10. **Strange mail (33–37):** mail arrives for "Pruitt Holdings Ltd." and 40 other companies. Their bank card is declined at the grocery store, during a triple-coupon run.
11. **The raid (37–42):** federal agents raid the house, led by Agent Halvorsen: crisp, by the book, and his questions keep drifting somewhere strange. The Pruitts are listed as directors of dozens of companies moving millions.
12. **The theory (42–45):** they're released pending investigation. Halvorsen is convinced Gary is a criminal genius: *"Thirty years of deep cover. Timeshares. Brilliant."*
13. **The TV talks (45–48):** a polite voice comes through the TV, asking them to sign transfer documents and return "the unit." It shows them a convincing deepfake of themselves confessing.
14. **MIDPOINT (48–50):** Donna reads the entire stack overnight. They legally own all of it, and there's a selection profile: prolific signers, long paper trails, no assets. Gary was chosen *because* he's cheap. His pride is shattered.

### 4. Squeezed (50–72 min)
15. **Caught between them (50–54):** Halvorsen wants them to wear a wire on bosses they can't name. The ring wants signatures and the hub.
16. **Kyle (54–60):** they drive back north. The resort is shut, but they find Kyle pitching at a different resort. His name is on the Canadian side of the paperwork, and he becomes an ally.
17. **The network (60–65):** Kyle explains that the TVs are nodes. They check on the circuit: some members' TVs are nodes and some aren't, and every node belongs to someone close to Polly.
18. **The coding club (65–68):** visiting Polly, they spot an old photo of her middle-school coding club. The kids' faces match the ring's avatars. They don't confront her yet.
19. **The crack (68–72):** Donna tells Gary he isn't saving money, he's afraid to commit to anything. They split up, and Gary is left alone with the humming TV.

### 5. Gary Pays Full Price (72–92 min)
20. **Polly (72–76):** Gary goes to Polly to ask for help rather than to accuse her. She confesses: they're her kids, and she has been protecting them since they were twelve.
21. **The circuit (76–80):** Gary gathers the circuit. Nobody here works for free, so for the first time in his life Gary pays full price, spending their savings.
22. **The clause (80–83):** Donna comes back with the clause. As legal owners, they can force an in-person signing to approve any transfer.
23. **The sting (83–90):** Polly pitches the ring like a timeshare closer: a "merger" that requires an in-person signing. She chooses a side, letting her kids face the consequences. Halvorsen's straight-arrow act finally cracks.
24. **Climax (88–92):** the circuit's nodes flip and the hub is rerouted. The nerds show up in person and sign without reading.

### 6. Paid in Full (92–100 min)
25. **Aftermath (92–96):** the Pruitts are cleared and Kyle is freed. Polly walks out with her head up. Halvorsen gets one last strange moment.
26. **Final image (96–100):** they check in to a real vacation, paid in full. The front desk offers a free gift for touring a timeshare. Gary looks at Donna and says no.

## Open questions
1. **The clause:** what exactly gives them leverage (forced in-person signing, dissolving the company, freezing the accounts)?
2. **The voice in the TV:** who leads the nerds? One of Polly's former students, presumably her favorite?
3. **The muscle:** a mob of nerds may have no physical enforcer. Is the threat drones, deepfakes and digital ruin, or hired muscle?
4. **Gary's payment:** does he spend Donna's hidden vacation fund? It would hurt more, and it would make the final image earned.
