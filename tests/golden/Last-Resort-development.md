## Logline
Two lifelong freebie hunters drive to Canada to claim a "free" TV for touring a timeshare. The paperwork they never read makes them the legal owners of a crime ring's laundering empire, and now the feds think they're the masterminds while the ring wants its company back.

**Tone:** Coen-style crime comedy · **Runtime:** about 100 minutes

## Spine
- **GARY WANTS:** Something for nothing, every time.
- **GARY NEEDS:** To see that his system isn't thrift; it's a way of never committing to anything.
- **DONNA WANTS:** One real vacation they actually pay for.
- **THEME:** Nothing is free; the fine print always collects.
- **CENTRAL QUESTION:** Can they get out of a deal they signed without paying the price?
- **ENDING:** They pay full price for something that matters, together.

## Movements and beats

### 1. The Circuit (0–12 min)
Pros at the timeshare game. Polly tips Gary off to the Canada TV.
*Audience feels: charmed, a little horrified*

1. Cold open: they dismantle a Branson timeshare closer and walk out with blacked-out cruise vouchers.

4. Polly tips Gary off: an 85-inch TV, pickup in person in Ontario. Gary sells it to Donna as "a vacation."

### 2. North (12–30 min)
A road trip run on cheapness. Kyle's sweaty pitch. Gary signs everything.

7. The TV doesn't fit in the car. They make it fit.

### 3. The Fine Print (30–50 min)
Strange mail, a raid, a voice in the TV. Gary was chosen because he's cheap.
*Audience feels: the floor dropping out*

9. The TV turns itself on at 3 a.m.; the router lights go wild. Gary calls it a bonus feature.

11. Agent Halvorsen's raid: they're directors of dozens of companies moving millions.

### 4. Squeezed (50–72 min)
Feds versus ring. Kyle as ally. The nodes point to Polly. The marriage cracks.

18. An old coding-club photo at Polly's: the kids' faces match the ring's avatars.
